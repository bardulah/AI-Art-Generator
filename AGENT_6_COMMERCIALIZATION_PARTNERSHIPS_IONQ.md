## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

IonQ is executing a **hybrid go-to-market strategy** combining government contracts (60-65% of revenue), cloud platform partnerships (AWS, Azure, Google), and direct enterprise sales. The company has achieved exceptional financial growth (222% Y/Y Q3 2025) through a multi-channel approach, though commercial success remains dependent on proving practical quantum advantage and diversifying away from government contracts.

**Key Findings:**
- **Customer Base:** 61% concentrated in top 3 customers; government contracts dominate (AFRL $54.5M, DARPA programs)
- **Partnership Ecosystem:** Established AWS Braket, Azure Quantum, Google Cloud integrations; significant strategic moat
- **Go-to-Market Score:** 7.5/10 (strong execution, concentration risk)
- **Revenue Model Sustainability:** 6.5/10 (hyper-growth masking structural dependencies)
- **Partnership Quality Score:** 8.5/10 (tier-1 cloud partners, but non-exclusive)
- **Commercial Traction vs. Competitors:** IonQ > Rigetti > D-Wave (IonQ commanding 21x revenue of Rigetti Q3 2025)
- **Confidence in Commercial Success:** 68%
