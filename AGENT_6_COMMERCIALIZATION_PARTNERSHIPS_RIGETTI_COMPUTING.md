## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Rigetti Computing operates a **PRE-COMMERCIAL RESEARCH PLATFORM** with minimal enterprise traction and heavy government dependency, not a commercial product with viable go-to-market strategy. The company generated only **$10.8M in revenue (2024, down 10% YoY from $12M in 2023)** with an estimated **$1.9M quarterly run rate (Q3 2025)**, placing it in the pre-revenue category despite a 10+ year operating history and public company status. Revenue concentration is extreme: **40% from government contracts** (AFRL, DARPA), ~25% from QCaaS subscriptions (AWS Braket, Azure, direct QCS), and ~35% from lumpy QPU hardware sales.

The **Quanta Computer partnership ($35M equity + $250M infrastructure investment over 5 years)** represents Rigetti's primary commercial lever, signaling recognition by a strategic investor that the technology requires extended development time and external distribution partnerships to achieve commercial viability. However, this partnership does **NOT** demonstrate product-market fit or enterprise adoption—it is purely a technology and capital partnership with deployment rights for Quanta in Taiwan/Asia markets.

**Key Finding:** Rigetti's "go-to-market strategy" does NOT exist in any traditional sense. Instead, the company operates a **venture R&D platform** with multiple revenue streams that lack sustainable growth trajectories. The path to commercialization (2028-2029 per CEO guidance) requires achieving quantum advantage in specific applications AND securing major enterprise partnerships, both of which carry 35-40% probability of success by 2028.
